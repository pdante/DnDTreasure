# Frequently Asked Questions

## Why don't paladins or rangers have cantrips?

In D&D 5e, paladins and rangers are *half-casters* — their spell list starts at level 1 and tops out at level 5. They don't get cantrips as a class feature. If you try to generate one, the app tells you the class has no spells at that level rather than rolling something wrong.

Artificers, also half-casters, **do** get cantrips. Their spell list spans levels 0-5.

## What's the Theme setting in 2024 mode?

The 2024 DMG groups magic items into four themes:

- **Arcana** — spell-oriented items (wands, scrolls, orbs).
- **Armaments** — weapons and armor.
- **Implements** — utility items and tools (boots, cloaks, bags).
- **Relics** — holy, cursed, or legacy items.

Pick a theme to limit the random pool. **Random** mixes them all.

## What does the ⓘ next to a magic item do?

Opens the item's description on **dnd5e.wikidot.com**, a community 5e reference.

- **Blue ⓘ** — a direct page exists; you jump straight there.
- **Grey ⓘ** — wikidot doesn't have the item indexed yet (usually 2024-DMG novelty items). The link still opens, but lands on wikidot's search. If the item is added to wikidot later, the link starts working automatically — no app update needed.

## How is the Tracker's "Magic Items Awarded by Level" target calculated?

Straight from the 2024 DMG. Targets per tier:

| Tier | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Very Rare | Legendary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (lv 1-4) | 6 | 4 | 1 | — | — |
| 2 (lv 5-10) | 10 | 17 | 6 | 1 | — |
| 3 (lv 11-16) | 3 | 7 | 11 | 7 | 2 |
| 4 (lv 17-20) | — | — | 5 | 11 | 9 |

Counts above target show in **red** as a soft warning. Your campaign isn't broken — just consider easing off magic items going forward, or accept that you're running a high-magic game.

## What's a "session" in LootForge?

A session is one play session — one game-night. Every magic item you commit to a campaign gets stamped with the current session number, so you can later look back and see what was awarded each session.

## Why did the app ask if I wanted to bump the session?

If 12+ hours have passed since the last commit in this campaign, the app assumes you're starting a new session and asks before stamping new items. Accepting bumps the counter; declining keeps the current session. Each campaign tracks its own 12-hour clock.

## Can I change the session number after committing items?

Yes — tap the underlined **Session N** in the toolbar.

- The new value applies to **future** commits only. Items already committed keep their original session stamp (you'll see the original number in each item row's `· Session N` tag).
- If you try to set the counter below a session that already has items, the app warns you — the "By Session" view ordering will reflect that history (later sessions on top).

## Why is my hoard giving so many Potions of Healing / Spell Scrolls?

The 2014 DMG's Magic Item Tables A-E heavily weight commons (potions, scrolls) at low CR. That's how the source material rolls. If it bothers you, switch to **2024 rules** — the new tables have a different distribution.

## I generated treasure but didn't commit it. Will it reset the session clock?

No. The 12-hour clock is based on the last **commit**, not the last generation. You can roll a dozen previews without affecting anything until you tap Copy and Commit to Campaign.

## Does Remove Ads sync across devices?

Yes, as long as you're signed into the same Google account on each device. After a reinstall or new device, tap **⋮ → Restore Purchase** to bring the entitlement back.

## I bought Remove Ads but they're still showing.

Try **⋮ → Restore Purchase** first. If that doesn't fix it, sign out and back into Google Play on the device, then re-launch LootForge. The app re-checks entitlement at startup.

## Where is my campaign data stored?

Locally on the device. No accounts, no cloud sync. If you reinstall the app or switch devices, you start fresh. There's no built-in export — if you want a backup, screenshot the Tracker tab.

## How do I report a bug or request a feature?

**⋮ → Send feedback** opens an email to the developer.
